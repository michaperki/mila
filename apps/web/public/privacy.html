Mila Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Mila helps you read Hebrew: you photograph or paste a passage, tap words to understand them, and save the ones you want to learn. This page explains what Mila collects, who it is shared with to make the app work, and how to delete it. Mila is made by Michael Perkins. Questions: mperkins1995@gmail.com.

What Mila keeps

  • Your account: your email address and your password, which is stored only as a one-way hash.
  • What you choose to save: passages saved to your library, saved words and the sentences you met them in, your practice history, and daily activity used to grow your tree.
  • Usage records: for each paid service call, which feature was used, when, how long it took and an estimated cost. These are used to keep costs in check and to limit daily use.
  • Reports you send: when you use the Report flag, your note and what the app was showing at that moment (the recognised text, the word explanation and the app version). Photos are never included.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes or hits an error, a report with the error, your device model and system version, the app version, the last failed requests and your account's internal ID (not your email) is sent to Sentry.

Mila does not keep your photos, does not use advertising or tracking, and does not sell or share your data for marketing.

Services that process your data

To recognise, explain and read Hebrew aloud, Mila sends some of what you give it to these services. They process it to return an answer to Mila; Mila uses their paid business APIs.

To save money and time, Mila keeps a shared store of answers (a word's explanation in a sentence, a table of forms, a recording of a sentence). These are kept by the text they answer, without any link to you or your account.

On your phone

Your sign-in is kept in the phone's secure storage, and your settings and some recordings are kept on the phone so they open quickly. Mila asks for the camera only when you use it to read a page, and for your photos only when you choose one.

Without an account

You can paste text and translate it without an account. Mila then counts requests from your network address for up to two days, to limit use, and keeps nothing else.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Account → Delete account. This deletes your account, saved words, passages, practice history, tree and activity, and issue reports straight away. Usage cost records are kept for accounting with your account removed from them. You can also delete just your saved words in Account, or write to mperkins1995@gmail.com to have your data deleted.

Security

All traffic between the app and Mila's server is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as hashes, and service keys never leave the server.

Children

Mila is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and it will be deleted.

Changes

Mila is in beta and this policy may change as the app does. The date at the top shows the latest version; significant changes will be mentioned in the app.
